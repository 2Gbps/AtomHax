The browser caps the game at 60 hz and redraws the stadium every frame. AtomHax lifts the ceiling and lets your display decide what is useful.

01 — Scripts

Give the game an open surface.

Load a plain JavaScript file, point it at the right realm, and read the match through a bridge small enough to understand.

02 — Room shell

Build the room around the match.

Favorites, room search, shortcuts, scripts, and chat tools belong around the field. Collapse the shell when the match starts.

03 — Preferences

Everything stays on your machine.

FPS limits, render scale, shortcuts, and scripts are saved in your app data. No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.
